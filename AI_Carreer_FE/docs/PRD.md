# PRD - Hanh Trang So (v1)

## Tom tat
Hanh Trang So la nen tang huong nghiep ca nhan hoa cho hoc sinh THPT va phu huynh, giup chon nganh - chon truong dua tren du lieu, dong thoi cung cap lo trinh hanh dong cu the va du toan chi phi.

## Muc tieu
- Dua ra goi y nganh/nghe va truong phu hop co giai thich ro rang.
- Cho thay khoang cach giua hien tai va muc tieu, kem lo trinh 6-12-24 thang.
- Giam rui ro chon sai, toi uu chi phi dau tu giao duc.

## Doi tuong nguoi dung
- Hoc sinh lop 10-12 (nguoi su dung chinh).
- Phu huynh (nguoi quyet dinh va chi tra).

## Gia tri khac biet
- Gap Analysis + Career Roadmap + Du toan tai chinh.
- Khong chi goi y, ma dua ra hanh dong cu the.

## Hanh trinh nguoi dung (MVP)
1. Trang gioi thieu va bat dau.
2. He thong bai test (MBTI + Holland).
3. Nhap du lieu hoc luc, to hop xet tuyen, khu vuc, tai chinh.
4. Tinh diem tuong thich nganh va giai thich ly do.
5. Goi y top 3 nganh.
6. Goi y truong phu hop theo diem, hoc phi, dia ly.
7. Danh gia kha nang trung tuyen.
8. Du toan chi phi hoc tap 4 nam.
9. Lo trinh hoc tap 12 thang (ban premium thu nghiem).
10. Bao cao tong hop (PDF).

## Pham vi MVP
- 2 bai test co ban (MBTI, Holland).
- Bo du lieu 50 truong dai hoc lon (diem chuan, hoc phi, to hop).
- Cong cu tinh tuong thich va goi y top 3 nganh.
- Bao cao PDF ca nhan hoa + roadmap 12 thang.

## Du lieu dau vao va nguon du lieu
- Dau vao: diem hoc tap, to hop, so thich, khu vuc, muc hoc phi.
- Nguon du lieu: cong bo tuyen sinh cac truong, thong tin hoc phi, diem chuan.

## Man hinh/Trang trong repo hien tai
- `src/app/pages/Landing.tsx`: gioi thieu va CTA bat dau.
- `src/app/pages/Assessment.tsx`: bai test.
- `src/app/pages/Results.tsx`: ket qua phan tich + nganh phu hop.
- `src/app/pages/Universities.tsx`: goi y truong.
- `src/app/pages/Roadmap.tsx`: lo trinh hoc tap.

## Chi so thanh cong (MVP)
- Ty le hoan thanh bai test.
- Ty le tai bao cao PDF.
- Ty le quay lai trong 7 ngay.

## Gia dinh va rui ro
- Gia dinh: du lieu tuyen sinh cap nhat hang nam.
- Rui ro: du lieu khong day du/khong dong nhat giua cac truong; can co quy trinh cap nhat va nguon tin.

## Ngoai pham vi (sau MVP)
- Tu van 1-1, tu van truc tuyen.
- He thong doi tac/affiliate tu dong.
- B2B cho truong dai hoc.
